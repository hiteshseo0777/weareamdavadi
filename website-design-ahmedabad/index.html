Your website is where every channel lands

Search, ads and social media all send people to your website. If it is slow, confusing or difficult to use on a phone, those efforts leak enquiries.

We design and build websites around a simple question: what does a visitor need to see and do before they get in touch? Because we also work in SEO, the site is built to be understood by search engines from the start.

Two ways to build

HTML/CSS or WordPress?

HTML/CSS websiteWordPress website
Best forBrochure-style business sites with a stable set of pagesSites that need frequent editing, many pages or a blog managed by you
SpeedTypically very light and fast because there is little extra codeGood when set up carefully, but depends on theme and plugins
EditingChanges are made by a developerYou can edit content yourself through the dashboard
MaintenanceMinimal, with no plugins or database to keep updatedNeeds regular updates and backups

We will recommend the one that fits how you actually plan to use the site, not the one that is easiest for us to build.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose HTML/CSS or WordPress?

If your site has a stable set of pages and you want maximum speed with little upkeep, HTML/CSS is excellent. If you want to edit content often or publish many pages, WordPress may suit better. We will advise based on your plans.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first and tested at several screen sizes.

Will the website be ready for SEO?

We build in the SEO basics, such as titles, descriptions, headings and structure. Ongoing SEO to compete for searches is a separate, longer-term service.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We would first review what ranks and works today so a redesign does not damage existing search visibility.

Who owns the website?

Ownership of the domain, hosting and website files should be agreed in writing before a project starts. As general good practice, these should be registered in the client’s name.
