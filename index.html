FAQ

Questions Ahmedabad business owners ask before hiring an agency

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency helps a business get found online and contacted by the right customers. That usually means a mix of SEO, local search, Google Ads, social media, website improvements and helpful content, planned around your goals and budget.

Which digital marketing services do you offer in Ahmedabad?

We are an SEO company and digital marketing agency in Ahmedabad, offering SEO, local SEO, Google Ads management, social media marketing, website design and development, and content marketing. Most businesses start with one or two and add others later.

How can SEO help a small business?

SEO helps people who are already searching for what you offer find your website on Google. When it is done steadily, it can become a reliable source of enquiries that does not charge you for every click.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It is usually a matter of months, not days, and the timing depends on your website, your competition and the work needed. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking or a date, so we focus on clear steps and monthly progress reviews.

What is local SEO, and does my business need it?

Local SEO helps nearby customers find your business on Google Search and Maps, through your Google Business Profile and local search work. It suits any business with a location or a defined service area in and around Ahmedabad.

Should a small business choose SEO or Google Ads?

Google Ads can bring enquiries sooner but costs money for every click, while SEO builds more slowly and keeps working over time. Many small businesses use a mix, and the right balance depends on your budget and timeline. Our guide on SEO vs Google Ads explains how to decide.
