Search campaigns that are set up carefully, tracked properly and improved steadily, so your budget goes toward genuine enquiries.

Paid visibility for people ready to act

Google Ads lets you show your business when people search for the exact services you offer. Done well, it can bring enquiries quickly. Done carelessly, it burns budget on clicks that never become customers.

Our approach is deliberately cautious: start with clear goals, set up tracking before spending heavily, and improve using real data.

When it makes sense

When Google Ads is a good fit

You need enquiries sooner

Ads can start sending visitors within days, unlike SEO which builds over months.

You want to test demand

Ads show which searches and messages people respond to before you commit to longer-term work.

You have a time-limited offer

Seasonal services, admissions periods or new launches suit paid search well.

What is included

Setup, optimisation and reporting

Scope is agreed in writing before work begins.

Campaign setup

Account and campaign structure organised by service, location targeting that matches where you work, and budgets you control.

Keyword and negative keyword planning

Choosing search terms with real buying intent and excluding irrelevant searches that waste money.

Ad copy

Clear, honest ads that match what people searched for and what your page delivers.

Conversion tracking

Measuring calls, form submissions and WhatsApp clicks so you can see which clicks lead to enquiries.

Landing page review

Checking that the page visitors land on is clear, fast on mobile and makes contact easy.

Campaign optimisation

Regular reviews of search terms, budgets, ads and settings, with changes explained to you in plain language.

How it works

  1. Goals

Honest expectations

What to expect

Costs, in plain terms

There are two separate costs: the ad spend paid to Google for clicks, and any management fee for our work. We recommend running ads from an account in your name, so you always own your data and can see the spend yourself. How we price management is discussed after we understand your business.

Realistic expectations

Results depend on competition, budget, your offer and how well your website turns visitors into enquiries. Early weeks are a learning period. We do not guarantee leads, sales or return on ad spend, and we will tell you honestly if ads do not look like a good fit for you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Enough to collect meaningful data on the searches you target, but no more than you can comfortably afford while learning. Typical click costs vary by industry, so we estimate this before you commit.

Do I pay Google directly?

Ad spend is paid to Google. We recommend an account in your name so you retain control and visibility of spending.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can begin showing within days, but it usually takes some weeks of data and adjustment before performance becomes reliable.

Can you guarantee leads?

No. Nobody can. We manage the campaign carefully and report honestly.

Should I choose SEO or Google Ads?

It depends on your timeline, budget and market. Our comparison article explains the trade-offs, and we are happy to give a view on your situation.
